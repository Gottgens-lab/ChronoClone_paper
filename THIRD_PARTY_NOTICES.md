## Analysis methods and resources

Palantir: Setty et al. (2019), doi:10.1038/s41587-019-0068-4. cNMF: Kotliar et al. (2019), doi:10.7554/eLife.43803. Hallmark gene sets: Liberzon et al. (2015), doi:10.1016/j.cels.2015.12.004. Gene-set files retain their source names and mappings; consult the respective MSigDB/Enrichr and library terms for reuse. They are not relicensed as original MIT code.

Frozen benchmark code and the historical ChronoClone wheel are scientific execution records, not a maintained package release. Upstream license files retained under `licenses/` and implementation provenance under `benchmarks/frozen_runtime/` govern those components. CLADES results refer only to the specified mouse-preprocessing implementation; the Cyclone diagnostic is limited to its documented abundance core.
